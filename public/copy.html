How to Copy and Paste a Message

Learn how to copy an SMS message and paste it into another conversation without typing it again.

Choose the device you are using.

1. Find the SMS message

Open your phone's SMS messaging app and find the conversation containing the message you want to copy.

2. Press and hold the message

9:41 ●●● ▰
A Messages · Alex
Hi! Here is the meeting address.
12 Market Street, at 2:00 PM

👆 Press and hold this SMS

Touch the message and keep your finger on it for one or two seconds until a menu appears.

3. Tap Copy

↗ Forward
☆ Star message

Tap Copy in the menu. The SMS is now copied and ready to paste somewhere else.

4. Paste the SMS message

Text message · SMS
12 Market Street, at 2:00 PM
Send SMS

Open another conversation. Press and hold inside the text box, tap Paste, then check the message before sending it.

1. Select the message

🖱️ Select the text

Use your mouse to highlight the message you want to copy.

2. Copy the text

Ctrl + C

Press Ctrl + C on Windows, or Command + C on Mac.

3. Choose where it goes

📝 Click the text box

Click where you want the copied message to appear.

4. Paste the text

Ctrl + V

Press Ctrl + V on Windows, or Command + V on Mac.

💡 Helpful tip

Copy makes a temporary copy of the message. Paste places that copied text somewhere else. The original SMS normally remains in the conversation.
